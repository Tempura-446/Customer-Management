How everything is organized

Customer → Project → (Measurements → Design → Quote → Invoice → Payment)

A customer is a person. A project (job number J-YYYY-###) is one job for them. Everything else lives inside a project, and each step links back to the one before it, so any invoice can be traced back to the exact design version and measurements it was priced from.

  1. Measure. Add a measurement set per room. Mark the source: on site or from customer. Customer numbers stay “not verified” and the app keeps reminding you until you check them on site, before you order anything.
  2. Design in 2020. Every time you change the drawing, save a new version (v1, v2…) and upload the .kit file, PDF and renders. Name the file J-2026-001_Smith_v2.kit. Link each version to the measurements it uses.
  3. Quote. Click “Quote this design”. The quote records which design version it prices. Once you mark it sent it is locked. To change price or scope, click Revise: that creates r2 and supersedes r1, so only one price is ever live. If you make a new design version after quoting, the project shows a warning that the quote is out of date.
  4. Accept. When the customer says yes, mark the quote accepted. That design becomes the approved design, and all other open quotes and designs are superseded.
  5. Invoice. Invoices are always created from the accepted quote: deposit (default {{ settings.default_deposit_pct }}%), optional progress payments, then the final balance. The app won't let you invoice more than the contract total.
  6. Get paid. Record payments against invoices. When the whole contract is collected the project moves to Paid. Close it when the job is done.

The project status moves forward on its own as you add things. The Dashboard → Next actions list tells you what's waiting: jobs to measure, designs to quote, quotes to follow up, deposits to invoice, measurements to verify, overdue invoices.

Files are stored on disk as data/uploads/J-2026-001_Smith/{measurements,designs,quotes,invoices,photos}/, so the folder structure matches the app even outside it.
